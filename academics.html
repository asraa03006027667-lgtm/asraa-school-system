Academic Progress

Assessment & Reporting

Regular assessment helps us understand every student's progress and support their learning.

01

Weekly Assessment

Regular weekly tests help teachers monitor academic progress and identify areas where students need support.

02

Term-Wise Examinations

First Term, Second Term and Third Term examinations provide a clear picture of academic achievement.

03

Student Portfolio

A complete learning portfolio records each student's academic work, development and progress.

Parent Reporting: Parents are kept connected through class-based WhatsApp groups, regular updates, assessment information and digital monitoring of academic progress.
