About Our School

School Introduction

Meaningful learning, practical education and future-ready skills for every student.

Asraa School System

Asraa School System is an educational institution based in Jauharabad, District Khushab, Punjab, Pakistan. The school was established with the aim of providing quality, concept-based and skill-oriented education and preparing students for the challenges of the modern world.


The school focuses on meaningful learning, Student Learning Outcomes (SLOs), Expected Learning Outcomes (ELOs), practical learning, character building, communication skills and future-ready skills.

Principal Message

Muhammad Bilal Hafeez

Director, Asraa School System

Muhammad Bilal Hafeez is the Director of Asraa School System, Jauharabad. He is focused on developing a modern, concept-based and future-oriented educational environment for students.


Under his leadership, Asraa School System emphasizes SLO-based learning, practical education, character building, communication skills and the development of future-ready students.
